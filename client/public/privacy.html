Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

BuzzBoard is a Discord Activity operated by StarrStride Studios. This Privacy Policy explains what information BuzzBoard processes, why it is used, and the choices available to users.

Information We Process

When you use BuzzBoard, we may process information provided through Discord, including your Discord user ID, username, display name, and avatar.

This information is used to identify players, hosts, and spectators during multiplayer games and to associate saved game boards with the correct Discord account.

BuzzBoard may also store content that users intentionally create or upload, including game titles, categories, clues, answers, Final Round content, clue images, and clue audio.

During a game session, BuzzBoard processes gameplay information such as scores, buzzer activity, wagers, answers, player roles, host status, and game progress.

Our hosting infrastructure may also process ordinary technical information required to operate the service, such as IP addresses, request timestamps, browser information, and server logs.

How We Use Information

Information is used only as necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and improve BuzzBoard.

This includes authenticating users through Discord, synchronizing multiplayer gameplay, storing user-created game boards, displaying uploaded clue media, troubleshooting errors, and protecting the service from misuse.

BuzzBoard does not require access to your Discord direct messages, server messages, contact lists, or private conversations.

Saved Game Privacy

Saved BuzzBoard game boards are associated with the Discord user who created them. Other BuzzBoard users cannot browse, load, modify, or delete another user's private saved boards through the service.

Discord Authentication

BuzzBoard uses Discord authentication to identify users. Discord authentication information is used to establish your identity and connect you to the appropriate game session.

BuzzBoard does not intentionally maintain Discord OAuth access tokens as permanent user profile records.

Discord's own processing of your information is governed by Discord's privacy policies and terms.

Sharing of Information

BuzzBoard does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate BuzzBoard, including Discord for authentication and Activity functionality and Railway for application hosting and storage.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, enforce our terms, or protect users and the service from abuse.

Data Retention

User-created saved games and uploaded media may remain stored until deleted or until StarrStride Studios removes them as part of service maintenance.

Temporary gameplay information is generally used for the active game session and does not need to remain as a permanent user profile after the session ends.

Technical logs may be retained by our hosting providers according to their own operational and security practices.

Your Choices and Data Deletion

BuzzBoard allows users to delete their own saved game boards through the application.

For requests involving additional stored content, account-associated information, or deletion questions, contact StarrStride Studios at starrstridestudios@gmail.com.

We may need sufficient information to verify that a request relates to the requesting Discord account before completing it.

Children's Privacy

BuzzBoard is intended for users who are permitted to use Discord under Discord's applicable age requirements.

BuzzBoard is not intended to independently collect personal information from children who are not permitted to use Discord.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through BuzzBoard. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as BuzzBoard changes. The effective date at the top of the policy will be updated when material revisions are made.
